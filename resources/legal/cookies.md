# Cookie Policy

_This is a template — edit it in **Admin → Legal** to match your service._

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This service uses only a small number of **strictly-necessary** cookies — the ones required to log you in and keep the site secure. Because they are essential to a service you actively asked for, they do not require a consent banner, but we list them here for transparency.

## Cookies we use

| Cookie | Purpose | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Keeps you signed in as you move between pages | Strictly necessary |
| CSRF token (`XSRF-TOKEN`) | Protects forms against cross-site request forgery | Strictly necessary |
| "Remember me" | Keeps you signed in between visits if you choose it | Strictly necessary |
| Cloudflare Turnstile (`__cf_bm` and similar) | Set by Cloudflare when our sign-in/registration CAPTCHA runs, to tell humans from bots | Security |

We do **not** use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, and we do not embed third-party social or marketing pixels. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

## Sign-in with Google or Facebook

If you use optional social sign-in, Google or Facebook may set their own cookies as part of their login flow, governed by their own cookie and privacy policies.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the strictly-necessary cookies above are required to sign in — without them the service will not work.
