# Data Deletion

You can delete your data from this service at any time. This page explains how — and, for
Google/Facebook sign-in, how to request deletion of the data we obtained from those providers.

## Delete your entire account

Sign in and go to **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and
everything tied to it — your providers, playlists, settings, and any connected Google/Facebook
sign-in links.

## Disconnect a linked Google or Facebook account

If you only want to remove a social connection (not your whole account), sign in and go to
**Settings → Connected accounts** and choose **Disconnect**. If a provider is your only way to
sign in, set a password there first so you don't lock yourself out.

## If you signed in with Facebook

Removing this app from your Facebook settings (**Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and
Websites**) automatically sends us a data-deletion request. We delete the Facebook connection we
hold for you and return a confirmation code. To delete your **whole account**, use
**Settings → Delete account** above.
